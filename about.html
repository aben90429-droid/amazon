The Story Behind Topazion

Honoring Generations of Ethiopian Artistry

Founded with a deep reverence for the weavers of Addis Ababa, Dorze, and Gondar, Topazion bridges century-old weaving heritage with the convenience of modern living.

From breathtaking festive Habesha Kemis adorned with intricate Tibeb patterns to lightweight, cloud-soft Netela scarves and comfortable homeware, every piece in our catalog is thoughtfully selected for durability, elegance, and soul.

“A good garment isn’t just fabric; it’s an heirloom of patience, cultural identity, and hands that know how to shape beauty from pure cotton.”

We work directly with artisans, ensuring fair wages, ethical work environments, and sustainable craftsmanship that preserves these timeless traditions for generations to come.

Customer Support & Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about sizing, international delivery, payment options, and caring for your cultural garments.

How do I choose the correct size for a Habesha Kemis?

Our Habesha Kemis garments feature both standardized sizing (XS through 3XL) and custom tailoring options. Each product detail page includes a comprehensive size chart with bust, waist, hip, and length measurements in both inches and centimeters.

For custom bridal or Melse orders, you can submit your exact height and measurements directly during checkout or by contacting our styling team.

Which currencies do you accept and how does conversion work?

Topazion supports live multi-currency browsing and checkout in US Dollars (USD), Ethiopian Birr (ETB), Euros (EUR), and British Pounds (GBP).

You can switch your preferred currency at any time using the Currency selector in the top utility bar or in the mobile navigation menu. Prices throughout the store will immediately adjust to your selection.

What are your shipping rates and estimated delivery times?

We offer worldwide tracked shipping. Domestic delivery within Ethiopia typically arrives in 2–4 business days. International express orders to North America, Europe, and the Middle East are delivered within 5–9 business days via DHL and FedEx.

Free standard shipping is provided on all orders over $100 USD (or equivalent in ETB/EUR).

How do I care for handwoven cotton Netela, Gabi, and Kemis?

Because our traditional garments are crafted from natural, hand-spun cotton and fine metallic thread (Tibeb), we recommend gentle hand washing in cool water with mild soap, or professional dry cleaning for embroidered bridal pieces.

Always line dry in the shade to preserve cotton vibrancy and iron on a warm cotton setting with steam.

What is your return and exchange policy?

We offer a hassle-free 30-day return policy for all standard catalogue items in unworn condition with tags attached. If an item arrives damaged or the fit isn't right, visit your Orders page or contact support to initiate an exchange or full refund.
